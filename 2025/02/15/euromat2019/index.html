Conference Title: European Congress and Exhibition of Advances Materials and Processes

Country: Sweden

City: Stockholm

Date: 2019-09

Presentation type: Oral presentation

Title: Pair interaction model of phase stability in FCC FeNi alloys

Authors: Yimi WANG, Frédéric Soisson, Charlotte Becquart

Abstract:

  • A temperature and concentration dependent-pair interaction has been established for FCC Fe-Ni alloys based on DFT calculations at 0 K and CALPHAD database at high temperatures.

  • Based on the constructed pair interaction model, Monte Carlo simulations have been made, obtaining thermodynamic parameters of the FCC FeNi system and the phase diagram.

  • The ordered phases L10-FeNi and L12-FeNi3 are stable, with respectively the transition temperature of about 684 K and 768 K.
